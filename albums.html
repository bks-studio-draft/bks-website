Our Albums

An album should not live on a shelf.

The day deserves more than a book you open twice a year and put away. So we design ours to be seen. To sit on the coffee table. To rest where guests gather. To become part of the home you are building together.

Our Albums

The Coffee Table Book.

For couples who want something grander, we make coffee table books in the spirit of a fashion or travel volume. Two to four hundred pages, bound at the scale of a book you would buy for its own sake, not a wedding album but a volume that happens to be about the best day of your life.

It lives in the open, where people gather, the kind of book someone picks up while they wait for you and keeps turning.

The book a guest reaches for and cannot put down.

Our Albums

Designed to be seen.

Every album is designed in a clean, contemporary style that lets the photographs speak for themselves. The spreads are custom, never templated, each one composed to carry its part of the story. The exterior is yours to shape, finished to match the rooms it will live in, or kept quiet and timeless.

The experience

An album, made with you.

When you are ready, one of our designers will sit beside you and build your album page by page, until it becomes the piece you reach for first when you want to remember.

An album is something you feel before you see it: the weight of the cover, the grain of the leather, the way a page turns. So we do not ask you to choose from a screen. You come to the showroom, sit at the table, and hold the albums yourself.

Our album team lays out the covers, the linens and the cases, shows you the Crystal Glance cover and the showbox with its built-in screen, and walks you through the finished heirlooms of couples before you. Then, together, we begin yours.

A designer shapes it page by page into something to cherish for generations to come.About the studio

Made to Be Seen

Not a keepsake tucked away.

Each album can rest in a showbox of its own, a modern object made to be displayed rather than hidden away. It can hold the album inside or stand on its own, and a few are built with a screen that plays your wedding film on a loop, so your photographs, your film, and the box itself become one piece in your home.

Wherever it sits, the intention is the same. Not a keepsake tucked away, but something on display, something that draws the eye the moment someone walks into your home.

Album Design

From your gallery to the heirloom.

  • Select Photos

    Easily select your photos via your online gallery or opt to have our album team select them for you!

  • Start Designing

    Once selected, notify our team to start designing your first draft and choose your album design preferences from our various layout options.

  • Touch Up

    Share any feedback on your first draft of the album via our interactive album design platform or in our studio.

  • Finishing Touches

    After revisions are complete and the layout is approved, you'll finalize the album title, colors, cover style, and complete the design of your physical album; the home of your memories.

  • Final Approval

    To ensure perfection, we’ll send you the final album draft to review to ensure that everything is perfect!

  • Album Production

    Our albums are handcrafted in our lab in Italy. Upon arrival to our Showroom, it will be inspected for perfection and then will be available for pickup or delivery.

Beyond the Album

Made for the walls of your home.

Your favorite frames are meant for the walls of the home you live in. The image that stops you every time deserves a place you will see it daily, a frame, a wall, a room where life happens. We design fine prints, framed pieces, and wall art built to last, sized and finished to suit the space they will hang in. A portrait above the mantel, a gallery wall along the stairs, a single quiet print by the bed. Years from now, these become the images your children grow up walking past, until one day they stop and really look. Ask us when you are ready, or explore the options in your online gallery.
