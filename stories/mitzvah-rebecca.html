From the Journal

Rebecca’s bat mitzvah, in spring.

A milestone close to home, with the whole family gathered around her.

A bat mitzvah is a family day above all, and Rebecca’s felt exactly that way. Spring light in the morning, grandparents in the front row, and a young woman standing a little taller by the end of it.

For us, these are the celebrations that show why we love being a family’s studio. We know the faces, we know who to look for, and we get to watch the story continue. We were honoured to be part of it.
