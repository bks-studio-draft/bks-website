From the Journal

Waterfront in early summer at Marina Del Rey.

The Sound on one side, the city skyline on the other, and a long June evening between.

Marina Del Rey sits right on the water, and the views do something to a wedding day. The bridge in the distance, boats on the Sound, the sky going pink behind the ceremony. We always plan a few quiet minutes outside with the couple just before sunset, and this evening rewarded us for it.

Alexandra and Sam were wonderful to work with. Relaxed, kind to everyone on our team, and fully present for each other. When a couple is that comfortable, the photographs come easily. We were glad to be there.
