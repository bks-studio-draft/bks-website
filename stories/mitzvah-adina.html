Adina’s Bat Mitzvah

A late-summer celebration in the most personal setting of all.

From the Journal

Adina’s bat mitzvah, at home.

A late-summer celebration in the most personal setting of all.

There is something special about a celebration held at home. The rooms already hold years of memories, and everyone moves through them with ease. We kept our team light and quiet so the day could feel exactly like theirs.

Adina was a natural in front of the camera, and her family made us feel like part of it. It is the kind of day we love to photograph: warm, close and completely unforced.
