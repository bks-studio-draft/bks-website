From the Journal

An autumn evening at The Rockleigh.

Turning leaves outside, a warm room within, and a couple surrounded by everyone they love.

We return to The Rockleigh often, and autumn may be its best season. The grounds turn copper and gold, and the evening comes in early and soft. We built the portraits around that window and saved the rest of the day for the people.

Sara and Harry’s wedding was full in every sense: full room, full dance floor, full hearts. With a team on every side we could stay with the couple and still catch the grandparents, the friends and the toasts as they happened. We enjoyed it from the first frame to the last.
