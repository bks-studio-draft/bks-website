Notes from the season.

The place, the light and what it felt like to be in the room. A few words from us with every story.

Oheka CastleLate summerAgnes & Moz

A late-summer evening at Oheka Castle.

Gilded rooms, formal gardens, and a couple who made the whole château feel like home.

Oheka asks a lot of a photographer. The staircases, the long allées of the gardens, the stone that turns gold as the sun goes down: every room wants to be the picture. Our job that day was to make sure the two of them stayed the picture, with the castle as the frame around them.

Read the story

The Lot at FormosaAutumnAleeza & Sammy

A Hollywood studio lot, made theirs.

We travelled west for an autumn celebration on a historic film lot in Los Angeles.

Travelling for a wedding always sharpens the senses. Los Angeles light is different: warmer, clearer, almost cinematic on its own. The Lot at Formosa, with its history as a working film studio, felt like the perfect stage for a couple who wanted their day to look like a movie.

Read the story

The VenetianLate autumnZoe & Noah

Chandeliers and candlelight at The Venetian.

A grand late-autumn celebration in one of New Jersey’s most storied ballrooms.

There is nothing understated about The Venetian, and that is the joy of it. Marble, crystal, sweeping staircases and a ballroom built for a full dance floor. A room like that needs a team placed with intention, so the scale of it never swallows the small moments.

Read the story

The RockleighAutumnSara & Harry

An autumn evening at The Rockleigh.

Turning leaves outside, a warm room within, and a couple surrounded by everyone they love.

We return to The Rockleigh often, and autumn may be its best season. The grounds turn copper and gold, and the evening comes in early and soft. We built the portraits around that window and saved the rest of the day for the people.

Read the story

Glen Island Harbour ClubWinterAlexa & Coby

Winter by the water at Glen Island Harbour Club.

A glass pavilion on the Sound, a crisp December sky and a room glowing from the inside.

The Sound was silver all afternoon, and by the time we reached the glass pavilion at Glen Island Harbour Club the light was low and clean. We took Alexa and Coby outside for a few cold, quiet minutes while it lasted, then brought them back in to the warm.

The GroveSummerJulia & Daniel

Garden light at The Grove.

Glass, greenery and a soft summer glow from the first look to the final toast.

The Grove is one of those places where the light does half the work for you. Walls of glass, trees right up against the windows, and that green, filtered glow that makes skin look its best. We planned the timeline around it so the portraits landed in the softest hour of the day.

Read the story

Old Westbury GardensJune 2026Lily & Elie

A June wedding, starting at Old Westbury Gardens.

Formal gardens, a grand old mansion and the first long days of summer.

Lily and Elie’s wedding was one of our first big days of the summer, and we started at Old Westbury Gardens. If you have been, you know: the sweeping staircases, the stone balustrades, the walled gardens. It is hard to take a bad picture there, so our job was mostly to keep up with the two of them and let it happen.

Rockleigh Country ClubHigh summerSamantha & Seth

A summer holiday wedding at The Rockleigh.

Lawns, long golden light and a celebration on one of the brightest days of the year.

Some weddings fall on a day that already feels like a celebration, and this was one of them. The Rockleigh’s gardens were green and full, the evening light ran long, and everyone arrived already in the mood to dance.

Marina Del ReyEarly summerAlexandra & Sam

Waterfront in early summer at Marina Del Rey.

The Sound on one side, the city skyline on the other, and a long June evening between.

Marina Del Rey sits right on the water, and the views do something to a wedding day. The bridge in the distance, boats on the Sound, the sky going pink behind the ceremony. We always plan a few quiet minutes outside with the couple just before sunset, and this evening rewarded us for it.

Marina Del ReyWinterTechila & Nachi

A winter night on the water at Marina Del Rey.

Early dark, city lights across the Sound, and a ballroom full of warmth.

In December the evening comes early at Marina Del Rey, and the water turns deep blue against the lights across the Sound. It changes how we work: fewer minutes outside, more attention on the glow of the room and the faces in it.

Beth ShalomSpringRebecca’s Bat Mitzvah

Rebecca’s bat mitzvah, in spring.

A milestone close to home, with the whole family gathered around her.

A bat mitzvah is a family day above all, and Rebecca’s felt exactly that way. Spring light in the morning, grandparents in the front row, and a young woman standing a little taller by the end of it.

At homeLate summerAdina’s Bat Mitzvah

Adina’s bat mitzvah, at home.

A late-summer celebration in the most personal setting of all.

There is something special about a celebration held at home. The rooms already hold years of memories, and everyone moves through them with ease. We kept our team light and quiet so the day could feel exactly like theirs.

A spring bar mitzvah at El Caribe.

A Brooklyn celebration with a dance floor that did not stop.

El Caribe knows how to throw a party, and the Idler family brought the energy to match. A big room, a packed floor and a guest of honour who handled it all with a smile.
