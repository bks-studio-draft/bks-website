From the Journal

A spring bar mitzvah at El Caribe.

A Brooklyn celebration with a dance floor that did not stop.

El Caribe knows how to throw a party, and the Idler family brought the energy to match. A big room, a packed floor and a guest of honour who handled it all with a smile.

We placed one photographer above the room and kept another close to the family all night, so nothing slipped by. A joyful day, and we were glad to be there for it.
