From the Journal

A Hollywood studio lot, made theirs.

We travelled west for an autumn celebration on a historic film lot in Los Angeles.

Travelling for a wedding always sharpens the senses. Los Angeles light is different: warmer, clearer, almost cinematic on its own. The Lot at Formosa, with its history as a working film studio, felt like the perfect stage for a couple who wanted their day to look like a movie.

Aleeza and Sammy trusted us with everything, and that trust is what lets us work freely. Our photo and film teams moved together through the day, so the stills and the motion tell one story. Being part of a celebration so far from home, and feeling so welcome in it, is something we will not forget.

We have put the photographs below in the order the day happened: Aleeza getting ready, the details, the ceremony, the two of them together, the room and then the party. Scroll through and you can walk the day the way we did.
