Zoe & Noah

A grand late-autumn celebration in one of New Jersey’s most storied ballrooms.

From the Journal

Chandeliers and candlelight at The Venetian.

A grand late-autumn celebration in one of New Jersey’s most storied ballrooms.

There is nothing understated about The Venetian, and that is the joy of it. Marble, crystal, sweeping staircases and a ballroom built for a full dance floor. A room like that needs a team placed with intention, so the scale of it never swallows the small moments.

Zoe and Noah gave us plenty of those small moments, the ones that only show up when someone is close enough to see them. We kept one photographer high above the room and another always at their side, and when the night was over we knew we had the whole of it. We were so happy to be there.
