From the Journal

A winter night on the water at Marina Del Rey.

Early dark, city lights across the Sound, and a ballroom full of warmth.

In December the evening comes early at Marina Del Rey, and the water turns deep blue against the lights across the Sound. It changes how we work: fewer minutes outside, more attention on the glow of the room and the faces in it.

Techila and Nachi’s wedding was all heart. Families everywhere, a dance floor that never emptied, and moments between the two of them that we were lucky to catch. A beautiful night, and one we enjoyed every part of.
