The engagement walk. Where it begins.

Of everyone involved in your wedding, no one stands closer to you than your photographer. That kind of closeness only works with someone you already trust, and this is what the engagement session is for.

Often a walk through a place that means something to you, it is an unhurried hour or two with no schedule and no one waiting on us. We learn how you move, how you stand when you forget to pose, how you look at each other when you think no one is watching.
