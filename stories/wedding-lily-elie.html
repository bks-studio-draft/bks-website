A June wedding, starting at Old Westbury Gardens.

Formal gardens, a grand old mansion and the first long days of summer.

Lily and Elie’s wedding was one of our first big days of the summer, and we started at Old Westbury Gardens. If you have been, you know: the sweeping staircases, the stone balustrades, the walled gardens. It is hard to take a bad picture there, so our job was mostly to keep up with the two of them and let it happen.

From there we followed the whole day, from the quiet minutes before the ceremony to the last dance. A few of our favorites are below, in the order the day unfolded.
