Julia & Daniel

Glass, greenery and a soft summer glow from the first look to the final toast.

From the Journal

Garden light at The Grove.

Glass, greenery and a soft summer glow from the first look to the final toast.

The Grove is one of those places where the light does half the work for you. Walls of glass, trees right up against the windows, and that green, filtered glow that makes skin look its best. We planned the timeline around it so the portraits landed in the softest hour of the day.

Julia and Daniel were easy to love. They laughed through the formal moments and went quiet in the right ones, which is exactly what we hope for. Our cinematographers stayed close through the ceremony while we held the wide view, and the room did the rest. A day we still talk about in the studio.
