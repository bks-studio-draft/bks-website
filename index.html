The Studio

A studio in the truest sense. Not one person with a camera, but a house of departments, each devoted to a single part of the day.

Booking, photography, cinematography, editing, and album design. The timeline is built with the planner before the day begins. The celebration and the quiet moments around it are captured together, freely and unprompted, so the day can be lived rather than managed.

The Studio Experience

How your day comes together when an entire studio is behind it.

The full experience

The Plan

It starts with a plan.

Before your wedding, our booking team builds your photography timeline hand in hand with your planner, so every hour is accounted for before it arrives.

The Engagement Session

Before the wedding, we get to know you.

Often a walk through a place that means something to you, it is an unhurried hour or two with no schedule and no one waiting on us. By the time your wedding arrives, we are not strangers with cameras. We are people who know you. An engagement session belongs to every collection we offer.

The Day

The room, covered at once.

Our team is positioned so the action and the quiet moments around it are captured together, nothing waiting on a single pair of eyes.

Secured

Secured the same night.

Before the night is over, your photographs and films are backed up to our own servers, protected from the moment they are captured and kept safe for good.

Delivered

In your hands right away.

A first teaser reaches you within a day of your wedding, with your full collection following in the weeks after, edited entirely in house by the people who know your day.

The Album

An album, made with you.

When you are ready, one of our designers will sit beside you and build your album page by page, until it becomes the piece you reach for first when you want to remember.
