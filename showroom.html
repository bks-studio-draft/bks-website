The showroom

From the street, to the table.

Chapter one

An open door, not a website.

Most of what we make is meant to be held. So we built a place where you can hold it before you choose.

The showroom is where the studio becomes something you can feel. Walk in and the albums are out on the tables, open, the way they will live in your home. Leather and linen covers, the weight of the pages, the showbox that holds it all.

This is where couples meet the team, build their collection, and come back after the wedding to design the album page by page with one of our designers beside them.

The album finished together in our showroom.From our philosophy
Your visitWhat happens in the roomIn person
  1. One

    Meet the team

    Sit with the people who will plan, photograph and film your day, and talk through every hour of it.

  2. Two

    Build your collection

    See the work in print and on screen, and shape the coverage that fits your celebration.

  3. Three

    Hold the albums

    Turn the pages of finished heirlooms, compare covers and cases, and feel the difference handcrafted makes.

  4. Four

    Design your album

    After the wedding, come back and design it side by side with our album team, until it is the one you reach for first.
