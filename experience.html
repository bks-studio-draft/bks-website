Where It Begins

The relationship begins long before the wedding day.

Of everyone involved in your wedding, no one stands closer to you than your photographer. We are there in the quiet of the morning, beside you as you walk down the aisle, and still there when the last song plays. For an entire day, we move through your most personal moments with you. That kind of closeness only works with someone you already trust.

This is what the engagement session is for. Often a walk through a place that means something to you, it is an unhurried hour or two with no schedule and no one waiting on us. You grow used to the cameras.

We learn how you move, how you stand when you forget to pose, how you look at each other when you think no one is watching. By the time your wedding arrives, we are not strangers with cameras. We are people who know you.

For many of our couples, it begins even earlier. They bring us in for the proposal, the engagement party, the moments long before the wedding is planned, and we become a small part of the whole love story rather than just the day it leads to. However yours begins, an engagement session belongs to every collection we offer.

The Wedding Day Experience

The day is yours to live. Every moment is ours to capture.

A wedding day moves faster than any other. Months, sometimes years, of planning arrive all at once and then begin to slip by in minutes. The morning becomes the ceremony, the ceremony becomes the first dance, and somewhere in the rush are the moments you will most want to keep: the held breath before the doors open, the friends gathered close, the room exactly as it was before anyone sat down.

The day is yours to live. Every moment unfolding across the room is ours to capture.

That is the reason we are there.

From the quiet of the morning to the last song of the night, our team moves through the day alongside you and around you. While you are fully inside one moment, we are watching the ones forming at the edges of the room. Nothing waits on a single pair of eyes, and nothing is asked of you beyond being present at your own wedding.

What follows is how we hold a day like this together.

Our style

Luxurious. Editorial. Timeless. Elegant. Classy.

That is how we photograph. Clean light, honest moments and an editorial eye, always with the final album in mind. We never lay a filter over your day. Your photographs look like your day, only better.

Our Technique

You never have to wonder whether a moment was caught.

Our photographers and cinematographers are there to tell the full story of your day, with warmth and a quiet, discreet presence. From the gentlest portraits to the height of the celebration, every frame is made with intention and artistry, always with your final album and film in mind. We bring a consistent eye to every part of the day, while letting each artist's instinct shape the images that become your memories. You never have to wonder whether a moment was caught. It already was.

Our Technique

Here with you, nothing left to you.

  • Here with you, for you

    Your lead photographer is beside you from the quiet of the morning to the final dance. Because your relationship with them began long before, at your engagement session, this is already someone you know, not just any photographer with a camera.

  • Nothing left to you

    The shot list lives with us, not with you. The moments that matter are watched and captured across the whole team, so every one is kept without a thing asked of you. You are free to disappear into your own wedding, certain all of it is being saved.

What We Offer

Why Benjamin Korn Studio.

  • A studio in the truest sense

    Not one person wearing every hat, but a house of departments: booking, photography, cinematography, editing, and album design. Each is run by specialists who focus on that part alone, giving a single wedding their full attention. It means the person answering your first email is not the one editing at midnight, and the one editing is not the one designing your album. Every part of your day is handled by someone who has spent years doing exactly that, and nothing rests on a single pair of hands.

  • Two crafts, one team

    Photography and cinematography, made side by side by people who work together every weekend and share one vision of your day. They move as a single unit, never competing for the same moment. Our film team works at the level of an independent studio, held to the same standard as everything else we make. Your photographs and your film come out feeling like two halves of one story, because they are.

  • Exactly as you imagined

    You have pictured this day for a long time. Our role is to capture it exactly as you experienced it. We work hand in hand with your planner so your décor, your details, and your timeline are photographed the way they lived in your mind, on schedule, and without a single thing falling to you.

  • Yours, fast and forever

    A teaser reaches you while the day is still fresh in your memory, often within forty-eight hours, sometimes the very next morning. From there, your full gallery and film are edited entirely in house, by the same people who were there, so nothing is lost in translation. Every frame is secured the same night on our own servers, never living on a single card, and in time it all becomes an album made to outlast the rest. Fast when you are longing to relive it, and built to last for the people who come after you.

The Details

We’re all about the details.

The dress before you step into it. The rings, the invitation, the small things you chose months ago and might never see again in the rush of the day. We photograph all of it, because memories live in the details.

Your Team

Built to its own scale.

No two weddings are the same. Every wedding is built to its own scale, with the right team shaped around the day it actually is. We will tell you honestly how many photographers and cinematographers your day calls for, never more than it needs, never less than it deserves.

Sooner Than You Expect

Your day, back in your hands.

The hardest part of a wedding is how quickly it passes, and how long you usually wait to live it again. We close that gap. A first teaser reaches you while the night is still vivid, often within a day. Your full gallery follows in the weeks after, your film soon behind it, each one edited entirely in house by the people who were there. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is sent away. It simply reaches you sooner than you expect.

Beyond the Album

Made for the walls of your home.

Your favorite frames are meant for the walls of the home you live in. The image that stops you every time deserves a place you will see it daily, a frame, a wall, a room where life happens. We design fine prints, framed pieces, and wall art built to last, sized and finished to suit the space they will hang in. A portrait above the mantel, a gallery wall along the stairs, a single quiet print by the bed. Years from now, these become the images your children grow up walking past, until one day they stop and really look. Ask us when you are ready, or explore the options in your online gallery.
